Quiet Tech

Today's Apps have become too loud. In an era of "attention economies," we build intentional, respectful tools that disappear when you don't need them.

01 No Data, Just Art

We believe in "Zero-Knowledge" utility. Our code performs its task locally on your device without ever needing to phone home. No tracking, no analytics—just a more beautiful device.

02 Digital Respect

Software should be invisible to your hardware. We prioritize native frameworks that respect your CPU and battery. If an app drains your device to serve an ad, it's not software—it's malware.

03 Affordable, Not "Free"

If you aren't paying for the product, you are the product. By charging a small, transparent price—less than a cup of coffee—we ensure our only shareholder is you. No catch, no data-selling.

04 Minimum Permissions

Privacy is a constraint, not a setting. We only ask for the specific system permissions required to do the job. You shouldn't have to wonder why an app needs access to your information.
