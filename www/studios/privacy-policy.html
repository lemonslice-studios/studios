Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 7, 2026

At Lemon Slice Studios, we believe that privacy is a fundamental human right. Our philosophy is simple: we do not want your data. This policy outlines our commitment to privacy across our website and our mobile applications, specifically Ambiance Wallpaper.

1. Our Core Principles

Our studio operates on four unbreakable principles:

  • No Personal Collection: We do not collect any personal information at all.
  • No Cookies: We do not use cookies on our websites, unless functionally required.
  • No External Tracking: We do not use external analytics, trackers, or third-party marketing tools.
  • No IP Storage: We never store your IP address anywhere.

2. App-Specific Policy: Ambiance Wallpaper

This section applies specifically to the Ambiance Wallpaper application for Android.

Data Access and Permissions

To function as intended, the app requires specific system permissions. Here is how we use them:

  • Set Wallpaper: Used exclusively to apply the chosen image to your device background.
  • Photo Gallery / Media Access: Used only to allow you to select your own photos for the Light and Dark mode wallpapers.

Crucially: All processing of images happens locally on your device. We never upload, transmit, or share your photos, metadata, or library information with our servers or any third party.

Storage and Persistence

The app stores your wallpaper preferences (such as the path to your selected images) locally on your device using Android's secure internal storage. This data is not backed up to our servers and is deleted if you uninstall the app.

3. Website and Analytics

To improve our services, we aggregate strictly non-identifiable browser information via our locally-hosted tools:

  • We track origin countries, device types, and screen sizes in aggregate.
  • We use fully anonymized hashes as same-session identifiers. These cannot be traced back to an individual user.
  • We host all our sites and tools locally, without the use of external hosting services or sub-processors.

4. GDPR and International Rights

For users in the European Economic Area (EEA), we comply with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Since we do not collect personal data, many of the standard "data processing" clauses do not apply. However, you maintain the right to:

  • Inquire about any data we might hold (the answer will be "none").
  • Complain to a supervisory authority if you believe our local-only processing violates your rights.
